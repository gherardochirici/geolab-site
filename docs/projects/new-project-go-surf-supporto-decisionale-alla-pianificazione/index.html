Project, 2019

GO SURF

Decision support for sustainable forest planning

On 16 September 2019 the kick-off meeting of the new project GO SURF, "Decision support for sustainable forest planning" (in Italian, "Supporto decisionale alla pianificazione forestale sostenibile"), was held at geoLAB in Florence.

The SURF Strategic Plan will use the Decision Support System to demonstrate to forest owners, land managers and forest-wood industry operators the possibility of initiating sustainable forest management that is better adapted to the complex problem of maximizing the production of ecosystem services.
