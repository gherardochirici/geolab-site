The new Italian Forest Inventory

Monitoring forest resources is vital for land management and shaping environmental policies. In Italy, a new National Forest Inventory has been launched, marking a major shift from previous efforts. Instead of occasional surveys, the new system provides continuous monitoring with yearly estimates. The statistical framework and survey methods have been updated, now using GNSS tools and integrating remote sensing data. This approach improves monitoring efficiency and allows for continuously updated maps of the measured variables.
