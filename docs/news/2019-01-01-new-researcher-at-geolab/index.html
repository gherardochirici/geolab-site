New, 2019-01-01

New researcher at geoLAB

The 1st December 2018 we hired a new researcher (RTDB) with a tenure track position of three years for Associate Professor at University of Florence. We are very proud to introduce Dr. Claudia Cocozza. Specialized in ecophysiology and especially in dendro-chemistry, and phytoremediation. But with some cooperation with remote sensing groups, see for example Battista et al. (2016) "Integration of Ground and Multi-Resolution Satellite Data for Predicting the Water Balance of a Mediterranean Two-Layer Agro-Ecosystem". Young, motivated, and higly productive (H-index 13), we hope to open together new research activities! Welcome Claudia!
