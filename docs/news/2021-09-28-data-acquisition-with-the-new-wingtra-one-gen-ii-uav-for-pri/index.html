New, 2021-09-28

Data acquisition with the New WINGTRA-ONE GEN II UAV for PRI.FOR.MAN Project

Two weeks ago Dr. Francesca Giannetti and Prof. Gherardo Chirici started the data acquisition in Regione Friuli with our new UAV "WINTRA ONE - GEN II" for the project PRI.FOR.MANShared PRIvate FORest MANagement in Eastern Alps

The flights we done where the firsts for our new drone that was acquired with the support and contribution of UNIFI and Regione Friuli.

We acquired photogrammetric data over the study area TAIPANA with our collegues of the University of Udine Prof. Giorgio Alberti and Luca Cadez. Here some of the beautiful images acquired with the the SONY RX1R!
