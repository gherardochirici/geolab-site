ForestSAT 2024 - Rotorua (NZ)

The Association for Forest Spatial Analysis Technologies (this is the full name of ForestSAT) was created to promote and advance the application of spatial analysis technologies in forestry.

For its 10th edition, ForestSAT 2024 was hosted by Scion in Rotorua, New Zealand.

Scion is a Crown Research Institute (CRI) for forestry in New Zealand and is at the forefront of driving sustainable forest management and innovation in the forestry sector.

Check the ForestSAT2024 conference programme here and don't miss the opportunity to (re)watch the two Keynote session and the Thomas Hilker Award ceremony on the ForestSAT Youtube channel https://www.youtube.com/@forestsat

As announced during the past conference, ForestSAT 2026 will be hosted by the University of Florida, Gainesville, USA - May 4-8, 2026. Visit forestsat2026.com for more details.
