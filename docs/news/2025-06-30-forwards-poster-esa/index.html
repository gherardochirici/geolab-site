FORWARDS Poster presentation at ESA Living Planet Symposium

WP2 updates in Vienna

Giovanni D’Amico, from UNIFI, presented his poster on

at the ESA Living Planet Symposium in Vienna. The poster, authored by partners Giovanni D’Amico (UNIFI), Saverio Francini (UNIBO), Ruben Valbuena (SLU), Costanza Borghi (UNIFI), Elia Vangi (UNIFI) and Gherardo Chirici (UNIFI), was related to the FORWARDS WP2 updates. It presented the relevance of producing a harmonised forest disturbance database and the first attempts to map forest vulnerability.
