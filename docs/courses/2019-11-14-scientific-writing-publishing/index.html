Course, 2019-11-14

Scientific Writing & Publishing

An international seminar aimed at discussing some of the most relevant issues related to scientific writing and publishing in peer-review journals. The seminar will focus on emerging opportunities and limitations offerend by open-access journals, the use of social-media in the scientific field, the management of international publications and the mechanisms related to the peer-review processes. The seminar is specially oriented to give advises and tips to PhD candidates and early-stage researchers to increase scientific productivity and performances.
