## LinkedIn

geoLAB took part in the MoniFun consortium meeting, held from 4 to 6 November 2026 in Brandýs nad Labem, Czech Republic, together with Italian colleagues.

For geoLAB, the main topics were next-generation forest mapping with remote sensing data and Federated Learning.

Federated Learning trains a shared model across several institutions without moving the original data out of each institution. National Forest Inventories can therefore contribute to a common model while the coordinates of their sample plots remain protected.

The partners also discussed the prioritisation of 95 forest multifunctionality indicators, on the way to a European Forest Multifunctionality Monitoring System.
