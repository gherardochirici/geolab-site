## LinkedIn

geoLAB, the Laboratory of Forest Geomatics of the University of Florence, takes part in NextGenCarbon, a Horizon Europe project on the terrestrial carbon cycle.

NextGenCarbon develops the next generation of terrestrial carbon cycle models by combining Earth Observation data, in situ measurement campaigns, vegetation demographic modelling and data assimilation techniques.

The aim is a better-informed assessment of carbon stocks and fluxes in terrestrial ecosystems: to track whether European ecosystems remain carbon sinks under changing climate, land use and management, and to support the update of global and European carbon budgets and EU climate policy.

The project started on 1 January 2025 and runs for 60 months, until 31 December 2029. It is coordinated by the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU), and the consortium includes 22 partners. The University of Florence takes part with geoLAB, in the Department of Agriculture, Food, Environment and Forestry (DAGRI).
