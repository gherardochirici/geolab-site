The <a href="https://aitonline.org/">Italian Association of Remote Sensing </a> (AIT), jointly with the <a href="http://www.irea.cnr.it">Institute for Electromagnetic Sensing of the Environment</a> (IREA) of the <a href="http://www.irea.cnr.it">National</a> Research Council of Italy (CNR), the <a href="https://geolabforest.blog/">Laboratory of Forest Geomatics at the University of Florence</a>, the Geomatics Research Group of the DISAFA-University of Torino, in cooperation with <a href="http://www.esa.int/ESA">European Space Agency</a> (ESA) and <a href="http://earsel.org/">European Association of Remote Sensing Laboratories</a> (EARSeL), and under the auspicious of <a href="http://www.gisig.eu/new-project-eo4geo/">EO4GEO</a> project organizes a Remote Sensing Summer School focused on <strong>“COPERNICUS Sentinels: Remote sensing techniques in forestry”</strong>.
