<img class=" size-full wp-image-243 alignleft" src="/media/2019/01/cocozza.jpg" alt="cocozza" width="182" height="174" />The 1st December 2018 we hired a new researcher (RTDB) with a tenure track position of three years for Associate Professor at University of Florence. We are very proud to introduce Dr. Claudia Cocozza. Specialized in ecophysiology and especially in dendro-chemistry, and phytoremediation. But with some cooperation with remote sensing groups, see for example <a href="https://doi.org/10.3390/rs8090731" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Battista et al. (2016) "Integration of Ground and Multi-Resolution Satellite Data for Predicting the Water Balance of a Mediterranean Two-Layer Agro-Ecosystem"</a>.

Young, motivated, and higly productive (H-index 13), we hope to open together new research activities!

Welcome Claudia!
