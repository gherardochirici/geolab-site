<p class="">The Association for Forest Spatial Analysis Technologies (this is the full name of ForestSAT) was created to promote and advance the application of spatial analysis technologies in forestry. </p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p class="has-text-align-center">For its 10th edition, <strong>ForestSAT 2024</strong> was hosted by Scion in Rotorua, New Zealand. </p>
</blockquote>



<p class="">Scion is a Crown Research Institute (CRI) for forestry in New Zealand and is at the forefront of driving sustainable forest management and innovation in the forestry sector. </p>
