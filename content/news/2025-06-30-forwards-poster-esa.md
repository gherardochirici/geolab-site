<h4 class="wp-block-heading has-black-color has-text-color has-link-color" id="h-giovanni-d-amico-from-unifi-presented-his-nbsp-poster-nbsp-on-nbsp">Giovanni D’Amico, from UNIFI, presented his <strong>poster</strong> on </h4>



<figure class="wp-block-pullquote"><blockquote><p><strong><em>Forest disturbances and vulnerability mapping: Preliminary results</em> </strong></p></blockquote></figure>



<p class="">at the <strong><a href="https://lps25.esa.int/">ESA Living Planet Symposium </a></strong>in Vienna. The poster, authored by <a href="https://forwards-project.eu/what-is-forwards/">partners</a> Giovanni D’Amico (UNIFI), Saverio Francini (UNIBO), Ruben Valbuena (SLU), Costanza Borghi (UNIFI), Elia Vangi (UNIFI) and Gherardo Chirici (UNIFI), was related to the FORWARDS WP2 updates. It presented the relevance of producing a harmonised forest disturbance database and the first attempts to map forest vulnerability.</p>
