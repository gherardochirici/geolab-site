From Monday 8th to Friday 12th April 2019 an international course on GIS and Remote Sensing organized within the ERASMUS+ Geonatura project, will be held in Florence. The course is aimed at 40 students from master's degree programs and doctoral courses of the 4 project partner universities: NOVA University of Lisbon, University of Florence, University of Delft, University of Glasgow.

On Friday 12th April, at 11:00 am in the Lecture hall of the School of Agriculture, will be held a project workshop entitled "Geomatics for environmental management" open to all interested parties.
