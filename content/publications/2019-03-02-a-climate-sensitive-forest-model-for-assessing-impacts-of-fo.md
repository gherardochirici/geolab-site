<img class="alignleft" src="https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S1364815218306753-gr1_lrg.jpg" width="432" height="435">A large international team working for three years at modelling European forests. This is in a very synthetic way the FORMIT project: <a href="https://www.wur.nl/en/show/formit.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">FORest management strategies to enhance the MITigation potential of European forests.</a>

Trying to predict in a rigorous way the effect of climate change to forest ecosystems production.

We find that none of the existing models was able to do this. So we decided to develop a new model: FORMIT-M.

Leaded by the group of Annikki Mäkelä who did a large part of this incredible jo.
