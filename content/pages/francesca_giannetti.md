<h3>Profile</h3>
<p>Francesca Giannetti graduated at the University of Florence where she obtained a Bachelor's Degree in Forest and Environment Science and a Master's degree in Management of Forest Systems (110/110), and at the University of Siena a Professional Master’s degree in GIS and Remote Sensing (29/30).  She obtained her PhD and Doctor Europeus in Sustainable Management of Agricultural Forestry and Food Resources from the University of Florence. She was visting Master Student in Swizerland at WSL and visiting PhD Student at Norwegian University of Life Science.</p>
<p>Her research interests deal with the use of  Remote Sensing data in operational forest inventory. I am interested in Sport (I was a professional Waterpolo Player and now I am a couch of Viola Swim Team swimming children team) and I love traveling.</p>
