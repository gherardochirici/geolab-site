<img class="  wp-image-378 alignright" src="/media/2019/10/img-20160218-wa00088491482987720510722.jpg" width="357" height="268">In our master degree in forestry at the course "Remote Sensing Laboratory" we introduce<span style="color:var(--color-text);"> basics of optical, microwave and LiDAR earth observation technics for producing useful information to support forest management and in</span>ventory.

Activities are carried out in the framework of the Copernicus Academy.

<img class="wp-image-380  alignleft" src="/media/2019/10/20190603_1454304219859638919889246.jpg" width="340" height="255">

Our method is based on a first course of Forest Inventory (first year of the master degree) where the students learn basics of design and model inferential statistics. They also acquire data in the field with a real forest inventory project. After the summer break they start to elaborate the data.

<img class="  wp-image-379 alignright" src="/media/2019/10/img-20160510-wa00032170716237167658811.jpg" width="359" height="269">

Small groups of motivated students are introduced to several tools and procedures in different environments. Including coding in R.
