The researchers of GeoLAB are involved in teching at the University Master "Management of Terrestial and UAV 3D Remote Sensing Data".

The master is coordinate by Prof. Grazia Tucci and organized by the Dipartment of civil and environment engineering and the department of menagement of agricolture, alimentary and Forestry systems of the University of Florence with the collaboration of Italian Military Geographic Insitute and the Collegio dei Geometri of Florence and the UAV flight center ZEFIRO.
