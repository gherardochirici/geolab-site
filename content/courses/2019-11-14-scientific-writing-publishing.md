<img class="  wp-image-387 alignleft" src="/media/2019/11/locandina_3dic_unifi.png" alt="locandina_3dic_UNIFI" width="300" height="630">An international seminar aimed at discussing some of the most relevant issues related to scientific writing and publishing in peer-review journals.

The seminar will focus on emerging opportunities and limitations offerend by open-access journals, the use of social-media in the scientific field, the management of international publications and the mechanisms related to the peer-review processes.

The seminar is specially oriented to give advises and tips to PhD candidates and early-stage researchers to increase scientific productivity and performances.
