<p class="has-black-color has-text-color has-regular-font-size">Do you work with <em>spatial data</em> and need to improve your skills? Are you involved in a project and need to manage a huge amount of <em>remote sensed data</em> but don't know how to do it? <em>This school is what you need!</em></p>



<p class="has-text-align-center has-black-color has-text-color" style="font-size:24px">Learn <em>coding </em>in <strong><span class="has-inline-color has-blue-color">G</span><span style="color:#db4437" class="has-inline-color">o</span><span style="color:#f4b400" class="has-inline-color">o</span><span style="color:#4285f4" class="has-inline-color">g</span><span style="color:#0f9d58" class="has-inline-color">l</span><span style="color:#db4437" class="has-inline-color">e</span> Earth Engine</strong> environment and become able to process Remote Sensing data at a global scale.</p>



<h2 class="alignwide has-text-align-center"><img class="wp-image-575" style="width: 900px;" src="/media/2021/04/SaveTheDate_GEE.png" alt=""></h2>



<p></p>



<p class="has-black-color has-text-color">Google Earth Engine Summer School will start on Monday 09/06 with an introduction by Professor Gherardo Chirici from geoLAB - Laboratory of Forest Geomatics. Then, the participants will form groups based on their skills and interests, to work on the realization of a GEE app until Friday 09/10.</p>



<p class="has-black-color has-text-color">From Tuesday to Thursday, from 9 am to 1 pm, lessons and practical exercises will focus on Google Earth Engine coding, images elaboration, remote sensing techniques, and more. Every afternoon, working groups activities will take place from 3 pm to 6 pm.</p>



<p class="has-black-color has-text-color">On Friday, an online open session will be held for everyone to attend. Moreover, students' groups will present their final Google Earth Engine application.</p>



<p class="has-black-color has-text-color">Ice-break, a social dinner, and a tour of Firenze will be organized for the students to enjoy our beautiful city.</p>
