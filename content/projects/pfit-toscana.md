<p>The geoLAB group is engaged in the development of the <strong>Territorial Guidance Forest Plans (Piani Forestali di Indirizzo Territoriale, PFIT)</strong> of the Tuscany Region, through the collaboration agreement between the Region and the Regional Research Centre on Forest Sciences (CReSF).</p>

<p>The PFIT provides medium- and long-term management guidelines for publicly, privately and collectively owned forest and silvo-pastoral resources, consistently with and in correlation with the other planning instruments in place (Art. 3, paragraph 3 of Interministerial Decree no. 563765 of 2021).</p>

<p>For each homogeneous area it identifies <strong>management guidelines and priorities</strong> for the protection, management and enhancement of the territory, specifying (Art. 3, paragraph 8):</p>

<ul>
<li>Management guideline (prevailing functions: protective, nature/conservation/landscape protection, productive, tourism/recreation, other functions) (Cartography)</li>
<li>Structural and infrastructural interventions, including upgrading, maintenance and planning of the road network (Cartography)</li>
<li>Forms of governance and treatment most suitable for the protection and enhancement of forest resources</li>
<li>Measures for the protection of biodiversity in Natura 2000 sites and protected areas</li>
<li>Specific rules of use contained in the landscape plans</li>
<li>Measures for the protection of sensitive areas, the management of natural risks and adaptation to climate change</li>
<li>Areas potentially usable for the creation of new forests (e.g. ecological corridors)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Cartographic annexes (Art. 3, paragraph 10, and Art. 6)</strong></p>

<ul>
<li>Land use designation, with recognitive value</li>
<li>Constraints on the territory, with recognitive value</li>
<li>Public and collective forest and silvo-pastoral properties and map of civic uses</li>
<li>Silviculturally homogeneous forest areas, with main management guideline</li>
<li>Structural and infrastructural interventions, including existing and planned forest and silvo-pastoral roads</li>
<li>Old-growth forests, monumental trees and seed forests, with recognitive value</li>
<li>Direct protection forests</li>
<li>Territorial and temporal framework (* MASAF Departmental Decree no. 64807 of 9/2/2023)</li>
</ul>
