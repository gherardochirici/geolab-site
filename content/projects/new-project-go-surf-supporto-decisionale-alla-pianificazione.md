<p>On 16 September 2019 the kick-off meeting of the new project GO SURF, "Decision support for sustainable forest planning" (in Italian, "Supporto decisionale alla pianificazione forestale sostenibile"), was held at geoLAB in Florence.</p>

<p>The SURF Strategic Plan will use the Decision Support System to demonstrate to forest owners, land managers and forest-wood industry operators the possibility of initiating sustainable forest management that is better adapted to the complex problem of maximizing the production of ecosystem services.</p>
