Europe's forests face a critical challenge: fragmented information hinders effective monitoring and management, and data silos limit the evidence available for climate and biodiversity policies. The EU-funded MoniFun project, which started on 1 January 2024, aims to co-create a harmonised European Forest Multifunctionality Monitoring System (EFMMS).

Through a multi-actor approach, MoniFun maps the information needs of different stakeholders, co-develops them into harmonised indicators with experts in forest multifunctionality, and co-creates a technical description of the EFMMS together with proposals for its permanent governance and funding.
